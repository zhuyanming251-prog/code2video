# SCRIPT.md

## Frame 1 — Hook
Couples fight all the time. Sometimes it gets ugly. Sometimes it even turns deadly. But it is not often that a man dies because his girlfriend, who weighed over 300 pounds, climbed on top of him and suffocated him with her own body weight.

## Frame 2 — Setup
This is the case of Keeno Butler, a 44-year-old man from Pennsylvania. He weighed about 120 pounds. His girlfriend, Windi Thomas, was also 44, but she weighed more than 300 pounds. Their relationship was messy, unstable, and full of alcohol, drugs, breakups, and arguments.

## Frame 3 — Warning
Keeno’s family already had a bad feeling about Windi. They thought she was dangerous. His sister even said she once warned her, almost half-joking, “Don’t kill my brother.” At the time, it probably sounded dramatic. Later, it sounded like a warning nobody took seriously enough.

## Frame 4 — The fight · knife
On March 18th, 2018, the two were inside their apartment in Erie. Windi had been drinking heavily, and reports say drugs were involved too. They started arguing. Then she pulled out a folding knife and cut Keeno across the hand. She also slashed him around the neck and face.

## Frame 5 — The fight · table leg
Keeno tried to fight back and get the knife away. But then Windi grabbed a loose table leg, with a bolt still sticking out of it, and hit him in the head.

## Frame 6 — Body-weight attack
By this point, Keeno was already hurt. He was a small man, around 120 pounds. Windi was over 300. And this is the part that made the case infamous. She climbed onto him near the couch, using her full body weight to pin him down. According to the case account, she positioned herself so that her body covered him, pressing down over his head, neck, and chest. Think about that for a second. A wounded 120-pound man, trapped underneath a woman more than twice his size, with no space to push her off and no room to breathe.

## Frame 7 — Death detail
This was not just being knocked down. This was suffocation by pressure. His chest could not rise properly. His neck was compressed. The medical examiner later said Keeno died from respiratory failure caused by blunt force trauma and compression to the neck and chest. Prosecutors said he had over 90 injuries. Knife wounds, blunt force injuries, bruising, and then the final pressure of her body weight.

## Frame 8 — Aftermath
And then it somehow gets even stranger. After the attack, Windi did not immediately call for help. She went to sleep. The next morning, she woke up, left the apartment, and reports say she went looking for drugs again. When she came back, Keeno still had not moved. That was when she finally called 911 and said the line that made the case unforgettable: “I killed my boyfriend.”

## Frame 9 — Investigation
When police arrived, they found Keeno dead on the living room floor. Nearby was the table leg, still with blood on it. Windi told police she had been drinking, using drugs, and that the argument had exploded into violence.

## Frame 10 — Court outcome
She later pleaded guilty to third-degree murder. Her defense leaned on alcohol, drugs, and chaos. But prosecutors described the attack as incredibly violent: the knife, the table leg, and finally the crushing pressure of a 300-pound body on a 120-pound man.

## Frame 11 — Ending
So the most disturbing part of this case is not just that she admitted it. It is how he died. Keeno Butler was not just attacked. He was cut, beaten, pinned down, and slowly suffocated under more than twice his own body weight.
